# Build plan: Ridgeline Walks (fictional demo)

The neutral test (A10). A fictional operator on the `expedition` archetype with a different font pairing, sharing nothing recognisable with Bakers except quality. Every image is a marked placeholder; every fact is invented and labelled `demo` in its `sourceUrl`.

## Archetype and theme

`expedition`, pairing `expedition` (Barlow Condensed and Source Sans 3). Deep green ink, warm parchment ground, rust accent. Topographic texture. Variants changed from the Bakers rebuild: hero `split`, positioning `left`, featureSplit `stacked`, offerCards `featurePlusTwo`, statsRow `cards`, testimonials `grid`, ctaBand `split`, keyFacts `pills` (eight of eight differ).

## Home page sections (9 of a maximum 10)

Hero, positioning, features, offers, trip finder, stats, signature map, signature moment, season calendar, testimonials, story, FAQ, journal, CTA, commitment, contact. Press strip skipped: no press mentions.
