Mount Feathertop is the one Victorian summit that looks like a mountain from every side. The Bungalow Spur is the classic way up: 11 kilometres through mountain ash, then snow gums, then open spur to the top.

It's a long day. We meet at 6:30 am, walk steadily, and are back at the pub by mid afternoon.
