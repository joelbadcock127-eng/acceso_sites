The Razorback is the finest ridge walk in Victoria and most people see it in a hurry. We take three days.

Day one is the ridge itself, day two is the summit of Mount Feathertop without a pack, and day three is the long green descent to Harrietville. Two nights are spent in our hut camp, with real beds, hot meals and a wood stove, so you carry clothes and water and not much else.

Groups are four to six walkers with two guides. Departures run October to April.
