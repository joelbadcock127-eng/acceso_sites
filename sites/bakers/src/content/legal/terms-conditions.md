## 1. Bookings and Payment

1.1. Bookings must be paid in full upon reservation to confirm your place.

1.2. Prices are listed in Australian Dollars (AUD) inclusive of GST unless stated otherwise.

1.3. Payments must be made via our secure online booking system. A confirmation email will follow successful payments.

## 2. Cancellation and Refund Policy

2.1. Guests may cancel for any reason, with refunds (or credits/date transfers where applicable) provided as follows:

(a) Multi-Day Walks. More than seven (7) days before departure: Full refund. Less than seven (7) days before departure: Full refund only if your spot is rebooked; otherwise, a 50% refund or a full credit for a future tour (valid for 12 months).

(b) Day Walks. More than twenty-four (24) hours before departure: Full refund or transfer to another available date (subject to availability). Within twenty-four (24) hours of departure: 50% refund assuming the spot is able to be filled, if not then no refund will be issued. Alternatively you may request to transfer to another available date (subject to availability).

(c) Corporate Bookings. More than fourteen (21) days before commencement: Full refund of payments made, less any non-recoverable third-party costs already incurred on the client’s behalf. Within fourteen (14) days of commencement: 50% refund, or a full credit (valid for 12 months) to reschedule (subject to availability). Where Bakers Walking Co. is able to rebook the vacated date, a full refund may be issued at the sole discretion of Bakers Walking Co. (less any non-recoverable third-party costs, if applicable).

2.2. Dissatisfaction during or immediately after the tour (within 24 hours) entitles guests to request a full refund. Requests are assessed in good faith on a case-by-case basis.

2.3. Bakers Walking Co. reserves the right to restrict future bookings for those suspected of abusing the refund policy.

2.4. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days via the original payment method (where available). Credits are issued electronically.

## 3. Amendments by Bakers Walking Co.

3.1. We reserve the right to alter itineraries or cancel tours due to adverse weather, tide conditions, insufficient bookings, safety concerns, or unforeseen circumstances.

3.2. In case of cancellation by us, guests may choose a reschedule, alternative tour, or full refund.

3.3. Each tour requires a minimum of six (6) confirmed participants and allows a maximum of eight (8). If the minimum number is not met, guests will be notified no later than seven (7) days prior to departure. In this case, guests may receive a full refund, reschedule to a later date, or transfer to an alternative available tour.
