# Copy log: Horizon Guides

## Voice profile (from their own pages)

Teresa writes warmly and directly, in the first person, to people she already walks with. Exclamation marks and ellipses are part of the voice ("makes the feet itchy!", "Come walking with Horizon Guides this season!"). She names places precisely (Cleared Ridge, Upper Portals, the Rabbit Board Fence, Kooloobano Point), grades honestly ("This is a long day and not suitable for beginners"), and keeps the ethic visible: "These are NOT fast-paced races to a destination", "No drones", "walk lightly and love your place". Site copy keeps that warmth and the place names, tightens the sentences, and drops the shouting capitals.

## Thesis (B3)

The one thing only Horizon Guides has: a guide who has walked the Scenic Rim's off track country since 2008 and interprets it as she goes, on small group walks that most people could never find alone.

Three hero options, all from intake facts:

1. **"The Scenic Rim, off the track."** Support: Small group bushwalks led by Teresa Cause, an eco certified guide who reads the country as you walk it. (chosen; Johnny asked for no year in the headline until the founding year is confirmed, since the home page says 2008 and the about page says 2007)
2. **"Walks that rarely feel a footfall."** Support: Guided day hikes and walking weekends in the Scenic Rim's national parks and private valleys, eight to ten walkers at a time.
3. **"Learn the bush by walking it."** Support: Eco interpreted bushwalks from Boonah, one hour from Brisbane, for beginners and old hands alike.

Facts used: established 2008 (home page; the about page says 2007, flagged in the gap list), Eco Certified since 2013 and Green Travel Leader 2023 (home page), maximum 8 to 10 walkers (day walk page), "places that rarely feel a foot fall" (winter weekend page), "Just 1 hour from Brisbane" (navigation retreat PDF).
