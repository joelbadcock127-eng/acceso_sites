Feel like you need a long walk in high places? The border country between Queensland and New South Wales offers many magnificent and intriguing walking opportunities. This three day adventure traces the watershed along the spine of the McPherson Range, taking in remote sections of the historic Rabbit Board Fence. It's a route that few people know, but the stories from history resonate at every turn and the modern settlers have tales to tell. The forests are lush and alive with bush fragrance, rich colours and birdsong, including the elusive Albert's Lyrebird.

The journey is vehicle supported, so you carry only a day pack with personal gear for each day. Camps are on private property where the group are the only guests.
