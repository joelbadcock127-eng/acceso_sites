Hello keen would be navigators! Don't find yourself discombobulated in the bush. Learn to navigate, practise the skills, and your world opens up.

Over four days the course covers map reading and interpretation, contours, gridlines, reading the land, using a compass, time and distance, route planning and learning to walk with the land. It also looks at apps and digital navigation and more advanced techniques like triangulation. The venue is Tuckeroo Homestead at Rathdowney, just one hour from Brisbane, with sunset views from the foothills of Mt Maroon.

The 2026 course has run. Request a place and Teresa will let you know when the next Queensland course is set.
