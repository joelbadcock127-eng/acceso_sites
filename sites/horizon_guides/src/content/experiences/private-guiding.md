Around Boonah there are wonderful places to explore nature on tracks and trails in the national parks of the Scenic Rim. The best places aren't always easy to find by yourself, because there are few marked tracks in this wilderness area. Having a guide along means your days in the bush are enjoyable and the focus stays on the experience and on learning about the ecology and history of the region.

There are walks of varying length and difficulty. Winter is the ideal time to explore the unique peaks of the Fassifern, and summer walks head for creeks where you can cool off in pristine rock pools and relax in natural isolation during the heat of the day. If you'd like a walk of longer duration there is a range of options to discuss.

If you want to go bushwalking somewhere special and would like all the arrangements made for you, get in touch to discuss the options. Teresa loves to create trips around individual requirements and will do all she can to make sure your trip is memorable for all the right reasons.
