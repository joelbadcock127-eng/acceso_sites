Horizon Guides has been sharing the Scenic Rim with walkers since 2008. The day long eco interpreted bushwalks are open to anyone with an urge to get out and experience the region, and beyond. Groups are small and the walks are spaced out through the season to allow time for recovery, and a bit of training, before the next one.

The walks are about enjoying being outside: observing the worlds about us, learning about the life we encounter, enjoying the views, breathing deeply and carrying some of that peace back home. They are also about learning how to bushwalk safely and managing yourself in different conditions with a small group of like minded souls. Hike regularly and over time you'll develop a great repertoire of skills and grow in confidence for future adventures.

Natural history interpretation is an integral part of every walk, so the group won't be galloping past the interesting bits. For safety you are expected to stay with the group. These are not fast paced races to a destination, and no drones are allowed under any circumstances.

Each season's walks are listed below with their date, area, distance, hours, grade and price. Some are on private property by invitation, with a small cash fee for the land manager. The program is subject to change for a variety of reasons, including adverse weather forecasts, at the discretion of the guides. Walks generally book out quickly, some within a few days, so don't take too long to decide.
