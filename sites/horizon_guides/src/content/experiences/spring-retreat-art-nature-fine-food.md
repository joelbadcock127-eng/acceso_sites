Take some time to be in the landscape and immersed in nature. Mt Maroon forms the dramatic and inspiring backdrop for two nights in country comfort at Tuckeroo Homestead, an introduction to the gentle art of nature journaling designed for beginners by nature connected artist Fleur Wade, and a Saturday dinner the group creates together with chef Caz Osborne of Scenic Rim Cooking Classes.

Nature journaling is for life. The benefits are many: wellbeing, health and the enjoyment of nature. Fleur is passionate about nature and will inspire you to seek out the stories of place and to make your own. Learning some art techniques helps you create a record through time of your wanderings. Remember, these sessions are not about being a great artist. You'll be in an inclusive and supportive group, so why not try something new and wonderful? Surprise yourself.
