Nestled in the heart of the Scenic Rim, beautiful Boonah is the base for a weekend of walking and delicious food. Three days of bushwalking in very different locations: step away from the busy regular tracks and let Teresa introduce you to the wonder of places that rarely feel a footfall. The walks are not a race. There is time to be in the landscape and immersed in nature.

Stay two nights at the renovated 121 year old Commercial Hotel, with Boonah Brewing Co on site, dine at award winning Blume Restaurant (named in the 2026 Gourmet Traveller Top 100 Australian Restaurants and 2026 Australian Good Food Guide 14 Hats) and breakfast at Arthur Clive's Bakery.

The inaugural weekend in August 2026 was fully booked. Request a place below and you'll be first to hear when the next one is set.
