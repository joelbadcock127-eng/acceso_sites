The Top End and Kimberley wet season is spectacular and, more importantly, enjoyable. This trip is designed to give you a taste of the best that the Kimberley has to offer at this amazing time of year. It starts with an easy acclimatisation and builds to a serious walk.

Gushing wet season creeks flow over countless waterfalls, through deep, cool gorges and lovely pools bounded by lush vegetation. Along the way there are numerous swims and time to simply relax and look at the nature around you. Unless you have walked in the wet season before, read the wet season notes before booking.
