A mix of day walks and overnight accommodation with extended walks to find birds that cannot be spotted near the roads. The Top End is a hotspot with more than 350 species recorded, six endemic to the region and many Australian endemics. With luck the trip finds some of the rare ones.

While this is primarily a birdwatching trip, it is also a nature trip and a bushwalking trip. You carry a full pack for a week on the main walk, because that is the only way to reach the areas where the birds are most likely to be. If you are uninterested in the rest of the environment and want only to tick off a list of species, this trip is not for you. For several weeks at the end of the Dry and the beginning of the Build Up, the Mamukala billabong becomes home to tens of thousands of birds, and this trip visits it.
