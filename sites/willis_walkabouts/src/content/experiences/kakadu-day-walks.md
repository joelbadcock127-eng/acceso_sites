A special trip for people who want to see some of the best that Kakadu has to offer without carrying a full backpack. Tours with some of Kakadu's Aboriginal traditional owners, the two cruises, all three of the most accessible art sites, a swim below a waterfall, and comfortable accommodation every night.

Special conditions apply to this trip, and additional expenses. Read the detailed trip notes before booking.
