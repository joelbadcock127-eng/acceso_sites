Walk the wonder of Kakadu in the Wet. Dry season visitors never see anything like this: every waterfall flowing, the landscape lush and green, and most of the swimming pools to yourselves.

With maximum temperatures in the mid 30s and nightly minimums in the mid 20s, the first section is an easy one to let the group acclimatise. It begins with the 300 km drive from Darwin to Kakadu and a night in accommodation at Jabiru, then builds through Burrungkuy, a two night camp above Baroalba Springs, the Guluyambi cultural tour, and an overnight walk to Kurrundie and lower Motorcar Falls that could not be done for ten years until 2023.

Section two leaves the road behind at Maguk. Pool after pool, waterfall after waterfall on Barramundi Creek, flowing at their wet season best. Additional expenses apply and the details are in the PDF trip notes, which you should read in full before booking.
