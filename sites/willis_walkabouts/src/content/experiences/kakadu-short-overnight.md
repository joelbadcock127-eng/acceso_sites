This trip is very different to the rest. Two separate overnight walks, each with two nights at one campsite, several day trips and overnight accommodation, the Guluyambi and Yellow Waters cruises and two Aboriginal cultural tours. It is the easiest trip on the program that includes overnight walks.

Koolpin Gorge is beautiful. The pools are perfect for swimming, the waterfalls and views a photographer's delight, and the art sites tell you this has been a special place for thousands of years. Please read the trip notes before booking.
