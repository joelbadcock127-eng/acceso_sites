This trip takes you into some of the most spectacular gorge country in Australia. The first section walks into Munjina and Dales Gorge; the second goes from Kalamina Gorge into Wittenoom Gorge and out at Weano.

White tree trunks against a background of red rock and a deep blue sky are everywhere. Some of the walking is quick and easy, some slow and scrubby, and the gorges are increasingly breathtaking. Years of exploring the area and trying different options have shaped the best hike, making the most of the time here. Some years offer excellent wildflower displays, others less so, but there is always something in bloom. The geology is as interesting on a small scale as it is on a large one, with outcrops of some of the oldest rock exposed on the continent.
