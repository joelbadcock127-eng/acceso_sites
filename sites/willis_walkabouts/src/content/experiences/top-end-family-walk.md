This trip has been designed to run at a slower pace than the other trips so that families with children can enjoy the Kakadu bush. Two trips fit different school holidays with the same itinerary; the one that books first runs.

The walking is very easy at times and more difficult at others, and the many swim stops make it far more enjoyable than a dry walk would be. The full loop at Motorcar needs a fairly steep climb; the shorter walks to Kurrundie Falls and Lower Motorcar Falls are relatively flat. If you don't have the patience to read the whole of the trip notes, this trip is not for you and your family.
